In Search of the Light

This book is not about cataloguing spirits or proving the unseen. It is a journey—a reclamation of what was silenced, forgotten, or erased. It moves through memory, ancestry, and the subtle forces that shape our lives. It bridges the visible and invisible, the inherited and the remembered, the lost and the reclaimed.

Beyond the Hustle

This preface introduces a book by a development finance practitioner with more than twelve years in the field. The author once firmly believed entrepreneurship was the answer to South Africa's unemployment crisis: talent was waiting to be found, funded and trained, so that township youth could build their way into an economy that had excluded earlier generations. The author no longer holds that view, and the book explains why and proposes an alternative.
